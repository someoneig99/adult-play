🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Play Suite provides simple browser-based games. This page explains how the current version handles information.

1. Information you enter

You may enter a nickname for the games or scoreboard. The current version does not ask for your email address, password, phone number or payment details.

2. Local storage

Scoreboard entries are stored in your browser's local storage so they can remain after a refresh. They are not automatically sent to the website owner. Other game prompts are generated in your browser.

3. Removing saved scores

Use the Reset Scores button to clear the scoreboard. Clearing this website's browser storage will also remove saved scores.

4. Cookies and advertising

This version does not intentionally set advertising cookies or include an advertising network. If advertising, analytics, embedded services or other third-party tools are added later, this policy must be updated to explain their data practices.

5. Third-party services

The site may be hosted by a platform such as GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, such as server requests, under their own policies. Review the policies of the services used to host or access this website.

6. Children's privacy

Players should use nicknames rather than real names or other identifying details. Do not enter private information into game fields.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy should be updated whenever the site's features, data collection or third-party services change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided by the website owner. A contact address has not been published in this demo.

This is a starter policy, not legal advice. Check that it accurately describes your published website and applicable legal requirements before launch.

📋 Terms & Game Rules

1. Friendly use

Use Play Suite for entertainment and friendly games. Treat all players with respect.

2. Participation is voluntary

Players may skip any Truth or Dare prompt or challenge without pressure or punishment.

3. Safety first

  • No dangerous, illegal or harmful challenges.
  • No pressure to share secrets, private messages or personal information.
  • No unwanted touching, humiliation or harassment.
  • Keep challenges suitable for the players and their setting.

4. Scores

Scores are for fun only. They are stored locally in the current browser and can be reset at any time. They do not sync between devices.

5. Availability

The games are provided for entertainment. Features may change, and uninterrupted availability is not guaranteed.

6. Responsible use

Do not use the site to bully, threaten, exploit or harm anyone. Stop playing if someone feels uncomfortable.

7. Updates

These rules may be updated when the website changes. Check this section for the current version.
